## Official handbook facts directly visible

- The official PDF title is “Approval Process Handbook 2024–25 to 2026–27”.
- Its index places “Chapter-II Grant of Extension of Approval for Existing Institutions” at printed page 11 and “Annexures” at printed page 77.
- The foreword describes simplification of EoA and reduction of some land-document and NOC compliance requirements. This is a warning against treating generic building/land documents as universally mandatory without reading the applicable clause and conditions.
